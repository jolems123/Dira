# Dira — Global Product Positioning

## Audience
Dira serves growing organisations of 20–500 staff with multiple buyers and approvers: schools, hospitals and clinics, hotels, construction businesses, manufacturers, distributors and NGOs, anywhere in the world.

## Promise
Procurement from request to audit trail, with clarity for buyers, approvers, finance teams and suppliers. Dira records payments but **never pays suppliers, holds funds, or moves money**.

## Product pillars
1. Self-serve procurement with transparent published pricing, a free tier and no setup fees; final prices require owner approval.
2. Full mobile workflow parity: request, approve, RFQ, quote comparison, PO, receipt, invoice matching and payment recording.
3. Multi-year departmental, project and cost-centre budgets with distinct commitments and actuals.
4. Basic stores and inventory, plus a catalogue that can capture lines from every stage.
5. Free supplier responses through secure, single-purpose, expiring invitation links without compulsory accounts.
6. International currency, locale and tax profiles with country-specific e-invoicing interfaces; no unsupported claim of live compliance.
7. Documented integrations, accessible UX, enforceable segregation of duties, isolation between organisations, and defensible audit trails.

## Competition and claims
Precoro and Procurify are product benchmarks, not a source of unverified advertising claims. Validate competitor pricing, mobile capabilities, setup fees and fiscal-year functionality at publication time. The public pricing page must not repeat unverified competitor assertions or invent subscription prices.

## Hard constraints
- Supplier payments are record-keeping events only.
- Plan limits are server-configured; **final plan prices: NEEDS ME**.
- Country tax rules are configuration and require jurisdiction-specific review.
- Uganda EFRIS and Kenya eTIMS stub integrations must never be presented as live.
- No production demo/seed data, destructive shared-database operations or edited applied migrations.
